Support & AMC

Annual Maintenance Contracts, with the scope written down.

Most IT contracts fail on what was never written into them. Ours states the covered systems, the response approach, what is excluded, and how renewal works — before you sign.

01

Comprehensive AMC

Parts and labour both covered, within the agreed scope.

  • Covered systems listed by name
  • Preventive maintenance schedule
  • Remote + onsite handling defined
02

Non-comprehensive AMC

Labour and support covered; parts billed as used.

  • Lower fixed monthly cost
  • Parts quoted before replacement
  • Same response framework
03

Support on call

No contract — per-visit or per-incident support.

  • Useful for small setups
  • Rates agreed upfront
  • Can convert to AMC later
04

Project / contract based

A defined piece of work with a start, an end and a handover.

  • Fixed scope agreed in writing
  • Milestone-based billing, no open meter
  • Documentation and handover included
  • Optional AMC from the day it goes live

FAQ

Questions we hear before the first meeting.

What happens in the free IT check?

You answer 16 plain-language questions about how your business runs — about three minutes, entirely in your browser. You get an Exposure Index and the findings behind it. Each finding quotes the answer it came from, and anything a browser cannot check is listed separately for an engineer to confirm on site. No sales call is required to start.

Is the check really free? What is the catch?

Yes — it runs in your browser and costs nothing. There is no catch: we believe the diagnosis should come before any recommendation. You decide if and when to talk to us.

How is pricing decided?

After the scope is clear — never before. We do not quote from guesswork: first the picture of your setup, then a written scope, then the price.

What is the difference between comprehensive and non-comprehensive AMC?

Comprehensive AMC covers parts and labour within the agreed scope. Non-comprehensive covers labour and support, with parts quoted before replacement. Both list the covered systems by name and use the same response framework.

Can AI actually help a small business like ours?

Yes — at the boring parts. AI is genuinely good at answering repeated customer questions, reading invoices and documents, drafting replies and summarising your numbers. It is not magic: we first map the workflow, show you a working demo on your own data, and where a simple automation is cheaper than AI, we say so.

An auditor gave us a VAPT report — can you just fix the points?

Yes — that is one of our most common jobs. We take the report, remediate it point by point on your actual network (firewall rules, patching, access, segmentation), and give you closure evidence for every point that your auditor, bank or client can accept. We also do the VAPT itself if you need one.

Do we have to replace Tally, Excel or WhatsApp?

No. Whatever you already use stays — we connect and improve it rather than replacing it. Replacement is only suggested when something genuinely cannot do the job.

Do you work onsite or remotely?

Both. One engineer owns your account and you meet that same person face to face — onsite in and around Mumbai, with remote monitoring and support running day to day across India.

What happens to the data I enter during the check?

It stays in your browser, in memory only — there is no server database, and the check sends nothing anywhere. Close or refresh the tab and it is gone. Read the full data & privacy note.
