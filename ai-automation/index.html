What this is

PureTechs builds AI agents and workflow automation for businesses, headquartered in Mumbai and delivering across India: WhatsApp assistants, AI document reading into Tally or Excel, quotation and payment follow-up agents, morning summary reports, email triage and custom agents on your own data.

Who it is for

Trading firms, distributors, manufacturers, clinics and professional firms where staff hours disappear into WhatsApp replies, follow-ups and manual data entry.

Problems this solves

  • Customers ask the same twenty questions on WhatsApp, at 11 pm, every day
  • Invoices and purchase orders are typed into Tally by hand, twice
  • Quotations go out and nobody follows up until the customer buys elsewhere
  • The owner gets sales and outstanding numbers only when someone remembers to send them
  • Everyone says "use AI" and nobody can say what it would actually do here

What is included

  • WhatsApp AI assistant that answers from your own catalogue and price list, 24×7, and hands over to a person when it matters
  • AI document reading: invoices, POs and bank statements extracted into Tally or Excel with a human check where it counts

Straight answers

Questions we hear about AI & automation.

Can PureTechs automate business workflows with AI?

Yes — WhatsApp assistants, document reading into Tally or Excel, follow-up agents, daily reports and custom agents on your own data, built around the tools you already use.

Can AI actually help a small business like ours?

At the repetitive parts, yes: answering repeated questions, reading documents, drafting replies, summarising numbers. It is not magic. We map the workflow first, show a demo on your data, and tell you when a simple automation is the cheaper answer.

Does our data go to a public AI service?

Only what the workflow needs, and with your agreement. Agents that must stay private are hosted so your data remains yours; we explain exactly where each piece of data goes before anything is built.

Do we have to replace Tally, Excel or WhatsApp?

No. We connect them. Replacement is only suggested when something genuinely cannot do the job.

How do we estimate the hours we could recover?

Use the hours estimate on the home page for a rough number, then let us map one real workflow — the mapped number is the one we stand behind.

How much does AI automation cost?

It depends on the workflow mapped, how many systems it connects to (WhatsApp, Tally, Excel or your own software), and the volume of messages or documents involved. We quote after mapping the workflow and showing a demo on your data, never before.
