What this is

PureTechs remediates vulnerability assessment and penetration test (VAPT) findings and compliance audit points for businesses in Mumbai and the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR), and carries out VAPT on networks, firewalls, Wi-Fi, servers and web applications where a fresh test is needed.

Who it is for

Businesses asked for a VAPT by a bank, an enterprise client, an ISO or regulatory auditor, or a partner; and businesses whose last audit produced a list nobody fixed.

Problems this solves

  • A VAPT report arrived with forty findings and nobody knows where to start
  • The auditor who tested does not fix, and the "computer wala" cannot read the report
  • The same findings reappear every year because they were never really closed
  • A client onboarding is stuck until the security points are cleared
  • Open ports, default passwords and unpatched services on the office network

What is included

  • Report walkthrough in plain language: what each finding means and what it risks
  • Remediation on the real setup — firewall rules, patching, access control, segmentation, application configuration
  • Prioritised plan: critical and high first, business-impacting changes scheduled after hours
  • Re-test of every closed finding with evidence: screenshots, configurations, scan output
  • Closure document your auditor, bank or client can accept
  • VAPT where you do not yet have a report — network, firewall, Wi-Fi, servers, web applications
  • Scheduled re-testing so the next audit is boring

How it works

Four steps, every time.

  1. 01

    Read

    Every finding mapped to the device or application it affects and the fix it needs.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Order of work, downtime windows and any hardware or licence needs agreed with you.

  3. 03

    Fix

    Changes made on the live environment, one at a time, with rollback ready.

  4. 04

    Prove

    Re-test, collect evidence, and hand over a closure pack finding by finding.

What we do not promise. Some findings cannot be closed without replacing end-of-life equipment or software; we will say so and quote it rather than mark it "mitigated". We do not issue compliance certificates — that is the auditor's role; we make the environment pass.

Straight answers

Questions we hear about VAPT & compliance.

An auditor gave us a VAPT report — can you just fix the points?

Yes, that is one of our most common jobs. We remediate each point on your actual network, re-test, and give you closure evidence for every finding.

Do you also perform the VAPT itself?

Yes — vulnerability assessment and penetration testing of networks, firewalls, Wi-Fi, servers and web applications, with a plain-language report. Where your bank or client requires an independent tester, we remediate and they re-test.

How long does remediation take?

A typical small-business report of 20 to 50 findings is closed in two to four weeks, depending on downtime windows and whether anything needs replacing.

Will the fixes break anything?

Changes are made one at a time, outside working hours where they touch production, with a rollback ready. That is why it takes weeks, not a day.

Can this be part of the AMC?

Yes. Scheduled re-testing, patching and firewall review under AMC keeps the next audit short. See IT AMC in Mumbai.

How much does VAPT remediation cost?

It depends on the number of findings, whether any hardware or software needs replacing rather than reconfiguring, and how many downtime windows are available. We quote after reading the report, never before.
