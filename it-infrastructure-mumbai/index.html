What this is

PureTechs designs, installs and manages office IT infrastructure across Mumbai and the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR): structured cabling, switches, Wi-Fi access points, dual-ISP internet, VLANs, and the server and storage layer — Windows Server, Active Directory, file servers and NAS.

Who it is for

Businesses opening or moving an office, adding a branch or godown, replacing a tangle of unmanaged switches and home routers, or outgrowing a single PC that has quietly become "the server".

Problems this solves

  • Wi-Fi drops in the far corner of the office and the internet crawls every evening
  • Cables run across the floor into unmanaged switches nobody can trace
  • One internet line — when it fails, billing, email and Tally stop
  • Files live on one PC under one desk, shared over a Windows share with one password
  • A new branch cannot reach the head-office server safely

What is included

  • Site survey and LAN/WAN design for a new office or a redesign, with a written bill of materials
  • Structured cabling — Cat6 / fibre, patch panels, racking, every port labelled
  • Managed switching with VLANs: office, servers, guest Wi-Fi and CCTV kept apart
  • Wi-Fi planning and installation — access points placed for coverage and capacity, dead zones removed
  • Dual-ISP internet with failover or load balancing, and ISP coordination on your behalf
  • Server and storage: Windows Server, Active Directory and user management, file server with permissions, Synology NAS
  • Handover pack: network diagram, IP sheet, credentials, warranty dates — on paper and in your documentation

How it works

Server & storage

The server layer, sized honestly.

A Windows Server with Active Directory earns its place when users, permissions and shared applications need central control. For many 5 to 30 user offices a Synology NAS with proper user permissions, snapshots and a tested backup does the job at a fraction of the cost — and we say which one fits before quoting. Either way: users and groups documented, permissions written down, the backup behind it tested, and the hardware from HP or Dell with a warranty date in your register.

Straight answers

Questions we hear about IT infrastructure.

Can PureTechs manage office networking and Wi-Fi?

Yes. Design, cabling, switching, Wi-Fi access points, ISP failover and ongoing management are core work, for a single office or several sites across Mumbai and MMR.

Do you set up Windows Server and Active Directory?

Yes — domain setup, users and groups, file-server permissions, group policy basics, and the backup behind it. Where a full server is overkill, a Synology NAS with proper permissions is often the honest answer.

How long does a new office network take?

A typical 10 to 40 user office is designed in a week and built in one to two weeks after materials arrive, depending on cabling and civil work.

Will guest Wi-Fi be able to reach our office data?

No. Guest Wi-Fi sits on its own VLAN with no route to servers or office PCs. CCTV gets its own VLAN too.

Can you connect our branch or godown to the head office?

Yes, with a site-to-site VPN over the firewall at each end, and a second internet line where the link matters.

How much does an office network or server setup cost in Mumbai?

It depends on office size and user count, how much structured cabling and civil work is needed, whether Wi-Fi coverage requires more access points, and whether a full server or a NAS fits the job. We quote after a site survey, never before.
