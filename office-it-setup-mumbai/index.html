Office IT setup in Mumbai A new office, working from the first day.

Moving into a new office in Mumbai, or relocating an existing one? PureTechs plans and installs the complete office IT setup in one sequence — internet, firewall, Wi-Fi, cabling, PCs, printers, server or NAS, Microsoft 365, backup and security — planned so staff can start work on the first day.

What this is

PureTechs plans, installs and documents the complete IT setup for new and relocated offices in Mumbai and the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR): internet lines and failover, business firewall, structured cabling, switching and Wi-Fi, desktops and laptops, printers, server or NAS, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, backup and security — one team, one plan, one handover.

Who it is for

Businesses opening a first office, moving premises, adding a floor or a branch, or setting up a godown or site office — typically 5 to 150 users.

Problems this solves

  • Five vendors for internet, cabling, PCs, printers and email — and nobody coordinating move-in day
  • Cabling laid by the interior contractor before anyone planned where the rack, printers and access points go
  • The ISP router quietly becomes the office firewall
  • Email, files and Tally have to move without losing a working day
  • Nothing is written down at handover — no diagram, no IP sheet, no list of passwords

What is included

  • Site survey and IT plan before the interior work closes — rack position, cabling points, access points, printer positions
  • Internet lines set up with the ISP, with a second line for failover where it matters
  • Business firewall, VLANs for office, guest and CCTV, and VPN for remote staff
  • Structured cabling, rack, managed switches and Wi-Fi access points — every port labelled
  • Desktops, laptops and printers set up, joined and tested; scan-to-email configured
  • Server or NAS with users and permissions; Tally moved and tested
  • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace with MFA; email and files moved from the old setup
  • Backup configured with a first restore tested; endpoint protection on every machine
  • Handover pack: network diagram, IP sheet, asset list, credentials and warranty dates

How it works

Four steps, every time.

  1. 01

    Survey

    Floor plan, headcount, move-in date, and what must not stop during the move.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A written plan and bill of materials: cabling points, rack, Wi-Fi, firewall, devices, cloud and backup.

  3. 03

    Install

    Cabling and rack first, then network and Wi-Fi, then devices, email, server and backup — in that order.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Everything tested, labelled and documented; optional AMC from the first day.

Straight answers

Questions we hear about Office IT setup.

What does a new office IT setup include?

Internet and failover, firewall, structured cabling, switches and Wi-Fi, PCs and laptops, printers, a server or NAS where needed, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, backup and endpoint security — planned together and handed over with documentation.

When should we involve an IT company when moving office?

Before the interior work is finalised. Cabling points, rack location, access-point positions and power are far cheaper to plan on paper than to fix after the walls and false ceiling are closed.

Can you move our existing server, email and Tally without losing a working day?

The move is planned around your working hours: email and files are moved ahead of time, and the server and Tally are moved and tested before staff arrive. The cutover window is agreed with you in the plan.

How long does an office IT setup take in Mumbai?

It depends on the size of the office, the civil work and the ISP’s installation date. The plan gives you a date for each step before anything is ordered.

How much does office IT setup cost in Mumbai?

It depends on the number of users and devices, the cabling points, whether a server or NAS is needed, the firewall and Wi-Fi sizing, and what can be reused from the old office. It is quoted after the survey from a written bill of materials — and an IT AMC can look after it from the first day.
