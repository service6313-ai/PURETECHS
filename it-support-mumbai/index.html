What this is

PureTechs provides day-to-day IT support for businesses in Mumbai and the wider Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR): end-user support for desktops, laptops, printers and scanners, Windows and Office applications, email setup and user troubleshooting, plus the network, server and security layers behind them.

Who it is for

Offices of roughly 5 to 150 users that do not have an in-house IT team, or have one person who cannot cover everything. Trading firms, CA firms, clinics, manufacturers, schools and new offices being set up.

Problems this solves

  • The "computer wala" comes when he is free, and nothing he did is written down
  • A printer that worked yesterday will not print today, and three people have stopped working
  • Outlook shows "disconnected", a laptop will not join Wi-Fi, a shared drive has vanished
  • New staff wait days for a working PC and email; departed staff keep their logins
  • Nobody knows which PCs are on which Windows version, or when the antivirus expired

What is included

  • Remote support first — most desktop, printer and email issues are fixed over a secure remote session without a visit
  • On-site engineer visits across Mumbai and MMR when hardware needs hands
  • Desktop and laptop support: Windows installation and updates, drivers, slow-PC clean-up, hardware diagnosis and replacement
  • Printer and scanner support: network printers, shared printing, scan-to-email, driver and firmware issues
  • Microsoft Office and Outlook support, email profile setup, signatures, shared mailboxes
  • User onboarding and offboarding: PC, email, Tally and Wi-Fi access created on day one and removed on the last day
  • Asset list and documentation: every PC, printer and licence recorded with its owner and warranty date

How it works

Four steps, every time.

  1. 01

    Ask

    You message on WhatsApp, email or the support form. An engineer replies, not a ticket robot.

  2. 02

    Fix remotely

    A secure remote session starts with your permission. Most user issues end here.

  3. 03

    Visit if needed

    Hardware, cabling and network problems get an on-site visit, scheduled by priority.

  4. 04

    Write it down

    What was found and what was done goes into your documentation, so it is not repeated next month.

Straight answers

Questions we hear about IT support.

Does PureTechs provide on-site IT support in Mumbai?

Yes. Engineers visit offices across Mumbai and the Mumbai Metropolitan Region — South Mumbai, the western and eastern suburbs, Thane and Navi Mumbai. Remote support covers the rest of India.

How fast is the response?

Remote issues are usually picked up the same working hour. On-site visits are scheduled by priority. AMC clients have the response approach written into their contract; per-call clients are served on availability.

Can you support our printers and scanners too?

Yes — network printer setup, shared printing, scan-to-email and scan-to-folder, driver and firmware problems, and print-server cleanup are all routine work.

We already have one person doing IT. Can you work alongside them?

Yes. Many clients keep a coordinator in house and use PureTechs for the network, server, security and escalation work, plus cover when that person is away.

Is IT support billed per call or monthly?

Both exist. Per-visit or per-incident support suits very small setups. A monthly AMC suits offices that want predictable cost and someone responsible. See the IT AMC page for how the two compare.

How much does IT support cost in Mumbai?

It depends on the number of users and devices, whether cover is remote-only or includes on-site visits, and how fast a response you need. We quote after understanding your setup, never before — see the IT AMC page for how per-call and monthly pricing compare.
