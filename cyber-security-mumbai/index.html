What this is

PureTechs provides managed cyber security for businesses in Mumbai and the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR): endpoint protection and EDR (endpoint detection and response), email security, MFA and access hygiene, patch management, security hardening, DNS filtering, security assessments and remediation, and incident support.

Who it is for

Businesses that run on Tally, email and shared drives; firms whose clients or banks ask about security before signing; any office where the antivirus quietly expired.

Problems this solves

  • Everyone knows the one password that opens everything
  • The antivirus expired and nobody noticed; a laptop has no protection at all
  • A phishing mail impersonating the owner asked accounts to pay a "vendor"
  • Departed staff still have live logins to email, Tally and WhatsApp groups
  • A client or bank sent a security questionnaire and nobody could answer it

What is included

  • Managed endpoint protection / EDR on every PC and server — deployed, monitored and renewed, not installed and forgotten
  • Email security: SPF, DKIM and DMARC verified, phishing and impersonation protection, attachment and link filtering
  • MFA enforced on email and admin accounts; break-glass accounts documented
  • Access hygiene: admin rights trimmed, shared passwords retired, joiner / leaver checklist
  • Patch and firmware management on a schedule for PCs, servers, firewall and NAS
  • Security hardening and assessment: exposed services, weak configurations, what an attacker would find first
  • Incident support: investigate first, explain in plain language, then fix and record

Straight answers

Questions we hear about Cyber security.

How to secure a small business network in Mumbai?

In order of return: MFA on email and admin accounts, endpoint protection on every machine, a tested backup with an offline copy, a business firewall with the guest and CCTV networks separated, patching on a schedule, and a leaver checklist. Everything else builds on those six.

Do you provide Microsoft Defender or another endpoint product?

Both. For Microsoft 365 Business Premium customers, Defender for Business is usually the sensible choice. Otherwise a managed EDR product is deployed and monitored by us.

Can you answer a client or bank security questionnaire for us?

Yes — we fill it from what is actually in place, fix the gaps it reveals, and provide evidence. See VAPT and compliance remediation when a formal report is required.

Our email got spoofed. What do we do?

Publish and enforce SPF, DKIM and DMARC on your domain, turn on MFA, and check for forwarding rules or logins you did not create. We handle all of it and show you the DMARC reports afterwards.

Is this only for large companies?

No. Most of what we deploy is built for 5 to 150 user businesses and priced per user or per device.

How much does cyber security cost for a small business?

It depends on the number of devices and mailboxes, whether endpoint protection or a managed EDR product is needed, how many staff need MFA and access hygiene work, and whether a security assessment is part of the scope. We quote after an assessment, never before.
