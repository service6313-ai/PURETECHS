What this is

PureTechs designs, builds and maintains custom software for businesses: internal tools and dashboards, client and vendor portals, web applications, Android and iOS apps, business websites, and Tally and ERP integrations. Headquartered in Mumbai, delivering across India.

Who it is for

Businesses tracking work in Excel and WhatsApp that have outgrown both; firms whose off-the-shelf software does not match the process; owners who want to see their own numbers without asking.

Problems this solves

  • Orders, dispatch or job status are tracked in WhatsApp groups and three spreadsheets
  • Data is retyped from one system to another because nothing talks to Tally
  • Clients or vendors call to ask for status that a portal could show them
  • A packaged product forces the business to work the software's way
  • The last developer left and nobody can change or even run the old system

What is included

  • Requirement discovery: understand the process first, then write code
  • Internal tools, admin dashboards and reporting built around your business
  • Client, vendor and staff portals; web applications
  • Android and iOS apps — staff, delivery or customer-facing
  • Tally and ERP integrations so data moves on its own; WhatsApp and Excel bridged
  • Business websites, e-commerce and catalogue sites — fast, mobile-first, hosted and handed over working
  • Legacy modernisation while the old system keeps running; maintenance and support after launch

How it works

Straight answers

Questions we hear about Custom software.

Can you integrate with Tally?

Yes. Reading from and writing to Tally is a regular part of our builds — invoices, ledgers, stock and outstanding flowing to and from portals, dashboards and WhatsApp.

Do you build mobile apps?

Yes — Android and iOS apps for staff, delivery teams and customers, connected to the same back end as your web tools.

Who owns the code?

You do. Source code, documentation and hosting credentials are handed over; the maintenance agreement is optional, not a lock-in.

How long does a custom tool take?

A focused internal tool is typically usable in four to eight weeks after discovery; portals and apps longer. Delivery is in stages, so you use the first part while the rest is built.

Can you take over software someone else built?

Usually, after a review of the code and hosting. We tell you honestly whether to modernise it or replace it.

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on the scope — an internal tool, a portal, a mobile app — the integrations needed, and the timeline. We do not price on a fixed number before discovery, because a price before understanding is a guess; we quote after the requirements are written down.
