What this is

An IT Annual Maintenance Contract (IT AMC, often called a computer AMC) from PureTechs covers your PCs, printers, network, firewall, server, backup and Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace under one agreement, with proactive monitoring, scheduled preventive maintenance, remote and on-site support, and documentation.

Who it is for

Mumbai and Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR) businesses of roughly 5 to 150 users that want one responsible party for IT instead of a different vendor for every problem.

Problems this solves

  • Three vendors, and every outage becomes a blame game between them
  • Patches, updates and backups happen only after something breaks
  • The contract says "IT support" and nobody can say what that actually includes
  • No monthly visibility: uptime, backup status and renewals are a surprise
  • Licence and subscription costs renew every month with nobody reviewing them

What is included

  • Covered systems listed by name — every PC, printer, switch, firewall, server, NAS and mailbox that is in scope, so a printer, desktop, network and server AMC is one contract, not four
  • Proactive monitoring: disks, backups, firewall, UPS and server health watched, with alerts before failure
  • Preventive maintenance on a calendar: Windows and firmware patching, antivirus checks, cleanup, health checks
  • Remote support for user issues, on-site visits for hardware and network work
  • Backup checks: every job verified, restore tested on schedule and written down
  • Licence and subscription review each quarter so you stop paying for seats nobody uses
  • Monthly one-page report: uptime, backup proof, open items, upcoming renewals

Straight answers

Questions we hear about IT AMC.

What does an IT AMC cover?

A PureTechs AMC covers the systems listed by name in the contract — typically PCs, laptops, printers, the network, firewall, server or NAS, backup jobs and the email platform — plus monitoring, preventive maintenance, remote and on-site support and a monthly report. Anything not listed is an exclusion, in writing.

Comprehensive vs non-comprehensive AMC — which should we choose?

Comprehensive covers parts and labour within scope, so replacements do not become surprise bills; it suits offices with ageing hardware. Non-comprehensive covers labour and support with parts quoted before replacement; it costs less per month and suits offices with newer, in-warranty equipment.

How much does an IT AMC cost in Mumbai?

It depends on the number of users and devices, whether a server or NAS is in scope, how many sites there are, how much on-site time is expected, and whether parts are covered. We quote after a survey, never before — a price without a survey is a guess.

Can the AMC include Microsoft 365 and the firewall?

Yes. Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace administration, licence review, firewall firmware and rule review, and backup checks are normally part of the same contract.

AMC or per-call support: what should a business choose?

If IT problems are rare and nothing critical runs on the network, per-call is fine. If email, Tally, billing or dispatch stop when IT stops, an AMC with monitoring and maintenance is cheaper than the outages it prevents.

Do we have to run the free IT check first?

No. Ask for an AMC quote directly. The check is useful if you want a picture of where things stand before the survey.
