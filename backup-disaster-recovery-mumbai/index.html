Backup & disaster recovery in Mumbai A restore, proven. Not a backup, assumed.

"Backup is running" and "restore works" are two different facts, and only one of them saves the business. For offices across Mumbai and the Mumbai Metropolitan Region, we build a 3-2-1 backup, monitor every job, test a real restore every month, and write down how long recovery takes.

What this is

PureTechs designs and runs business backup and disaster recovery for offices in Mumbai and the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR): server, PC, Tally and file backup to a NAS, an offline or immutable copy for ransomware, a cloud copy for site loss, backup monitoring, scheduled restore testing and a written recovery plan.

Who it is for

Any business whose Tally data, files or email cannot be re-created — which is every business — and especially those that have never once tested a restore.

Problems this solves

  • Backup "is running" to an external drive that stays plugged into the same PC
  • A restore has never been tested, or was tested years ago on different systems
  • Ransomware would encrypt the backup along with the data
  • Tally data lives on one machine with no copy outside the office
  • Nobody can say how long it would take to get the business working again

What is included

  • Backup design on the 3-2-1 principle: three copies, two media, one off site
  • Nightly backup of servers, PCs, Tally and files to a NAS with retention and versioning
  • Offline or immutable copy so a ransomware attack becomes an inconvenience, not a shutdown
  • Cloud copy with versioning for fire, flood, theft or a failed NAS
  • Backup monitoring: every job checked, failures alerted, not discovered months later
  • Monthly restore test of real files, timed and recorded; annual full recovery drill for AMC clients
  • Written disaster recovery plan: what is restored first, by whom, in how long

Straight answers

Questions we hear about Backup & disaster recovery.

Can PureTechs handle backup and disaster recovery?

Yes — design, deployment, monitoring, restore testing and a written recovery plan, for offices across Mumbai and MMR, using Synology NAS, Veeam or Iperius and a cloud copy as fits the scope.

How often should business backups be tested?

A restore of real files should be tested monthly and recorded, and a full recovery of the main server or Tally data drilled at least once a year. A backup that has never been restored is a belief, not a control.

What is a 3-2-1 backup?

Three copies of your data, on two different types of storage, with one copy off site. In practice: the live data, a NAS in the office, and an offline or cloud copy that ransomware cannot reach.

Is backup to an external hard drive enough?

No. A drive that stays connected is encrypted along with everything else, and a drive that leaves the office is rarely rotated. It can be one copy of three, never the only one.

Do you back up Tally?

Yes. Tally data folders are backed up nightly with versioning, and restore is tested by opening the restored company in Tally, not just by checking the file exists.

How much does backup and disaster recovery cost?

It depends on how much data there is, how many servers and PCs are covered, whether an offline or cloud copy is needed, and how often restores are tested. We quote after an inventory of what exists, never before.
